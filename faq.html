Frequently asked questions

Answers before you place an order.

Still have a question? Contact us and we'll help you determine the best workflow.

How do I get a quote?

Email us with the service, approximate image count, complexity, deadline, and a few representative images or a file link. We'll review the requirements and respond with pricing.

Do you offer a free sample?

Yes. New customers can request up to two free sample images so they can review the quality before placing a larger order.

What file formats can you work with?

We can work with common image formats such as JPG, PNG, TIFF, and PSD. Tell us your required delivery format when requesting a quote.

Do you accept bulk orders?

Yes. Bulk and recurring image editing is a core part of the service. Send the approximate volume and we'll prepare a custom quote.

How fast can you deliver?

Turnaround depends on service complexity and image volume. We'll confirm the expected delivery time before starting the project.

Can you handle recurring monthly work?

Yes. We can discuss a recurring workflow for agencies, photographers, retailers, and e-commerce businesses.

How do I send large files?

For the initial inquiry, you can email a link to your preferred file-sharing service. We can then coordinate the delivery workflow.

Do you provide revisions?

If an edit does not match the approved instructions, contact us with the specific issue and we'll review it.

Do you work with clients outside the USA and Canada?

Yes. Clipping Path Studio serves clients worldwide.

Are the portfolio images on this website real client work?

The site is being prepared with clearly marked placeholders. Replace them with your own work and authorized examples before publishing them as your portfolio.
